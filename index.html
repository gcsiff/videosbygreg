A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Greg Csiffary

Videographer, editor, visual memory collector

Videography is a passion not just a profession for me. I love to be out on the field and I also like creating a narrative during editing.

A freelance videographer for 6+ years now, I was fortunate to capture diverse topics all over the world — from making wedding videos in Iceland, following dance companies on their European tour, filming innovative buildings, to capturing branded and corporate events in CEE.
